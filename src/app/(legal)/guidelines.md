# Community Guidelines

Opinionly exists to help people grow through honest, anonymous feedback from the people who know them. That only works when the community stays kind, constructive, and safe. These Community Guidelines explain the standards every member agrees to. They are part of, and incorporated into, our [Terms of Service](/terms) and [End-User License Agreement](/eula) — by using Opinionly you agree to follow them.

## What Opinionly is for

- Giving thoughtful, specific feedback that helps someone improve.
- Receiving feedback with an open mind, even when it is hard to hear.
- Building a supportive culture where honesty and respect go together.

## What we encourage

- **Be constructive.** Focus on behavior and growth, not personal attacks.
- **Be honest, but kind.** Anonymity is a tool for candor, not cruelty.
- **Be specific.** Useful feedback points to real examples and actionable change.
- **Assume good intent.** Most people are here to help and to grow.

## What is not allowed

We have **zero tolerance** for objectionable content and abusive behavior. You may not submit, post, or share content that:

- Is obscene, lewd, lascivious, filthy, violent, harassing, libelous, slanderous, or otherwise objectionable.
- Ridicules, mocks, disparages, intimidates, bullies, or abuses any person.
- Harasses or threatens any person, or promotes violence against a person or group.
- Includes hateful or discriminatory remarks connected to race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any protected characteristic.
- Sexually exploits or endangers minors in any way, or otherwise violates laws protecting the health or well-being of minors.
- Violates the privacy or publicity rights of any third party, or discloses someone's private information.
- Is false, inaccurate, or deliberately misleading, or impersonates another person.
- Is unsolicited advertising, spam, promotional material, or any form of solicitation.
- Violates any applicable law, regulation, or rule, or links to material that does.

## Reporting and blocking

If you see content that violates these Guidelines, or you want to stop interacting with someone, you can act directly in the app:

- **Report content** from the feedback you received so our team can review it.
- **Block a user** to immediately stop receiving feedback or messages from them.

Reports are confidential. You will never be required to confront the person you are reporting.

## How we enforce these Guidelines

We review every report. When content violates these Guidelines, we will **remove the violating content and may eject the responsible user** — typically **within 24 hours** of a report. Depending on severity, consequences may include a warning, removal of content, suspension, or permanent termination of access, at our discretion and without notice. Serious violations, such as content that endangers minors or threatens violence, may be reported to law enforcement.
